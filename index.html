privacy policy

last updated: 13 august 2026

you're beautiful does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

the app runs entirely on your device. it has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. nothing you do in the app ever leaves your phone.

because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete, and no data is ever sold or shared with third parties.

if apple's operating system provides anonymous, aggregated crash or usage statistics through the app store, those are handled by apple under apple's own privacy policy and are not personally identifying.

this policy may be updated if the app changes. any update will be posted at this same address.
